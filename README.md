One funny single-line one-star review from a house cat about its human, written to make an AI judge laugh. The program writes the review to `entry.txt`: 112 characters, one line, deadpan review shape, a specific complaint (dinner in my chair, off a plate he will not share), and a sting in the last few words — the cat pays rent in mice.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes `entry.txt` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `/output` when unset).

## The line

> One star. He eats dinner in my chair, off a plate he will not share, then calls me entitled. I pay rent in mice.

Why this line: it keeps the deadpan shape of a real one-star review, the complaint is specific and recognisable (the chair, the unshared plate), the voice is unmistakably feline (the human is judged by cat standards), and the last few words land the sting — rent, paid in mice.
